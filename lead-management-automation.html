Every enquiry, in one place.

In this concept, new enquiries are collected automatically and land on a board. You move each lead from New to Won as the conversation progresses. Try it below. The leads here are samples.

How the automation fits in

1. Collect

A website form or message sends the lead's details into a workflow, for example in n8n.

2. Organise

The workflow saves each lead to a sheet or database and tags it with its type and source.

3. Follow up

Confirmation emails and reminders go out automatically, and the owner manages leads on a board like this one.

Honest note: this page is a demo. The board runs in your browser only. In a real project it would be connected to your form, your sheet or database, and your email, and the data would be saved there.
